Privacy

This site is static: plain HTML and CSS, with no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking script of any kind. The only requests a page makes to a third party are the contact form's and the newsletter form's submissions, described below.

IZZR Labs itself does not collect or store anything about you just from visiting. GitHub Pages, like any web host, keeps IP addresses and user-agent strings in its own server logs — that's a GitHub log, not something IZZR Labs has access to or stores separately.

What you type into the contact form is sent to Formspree, a third-party form service, and arrives as an email. It's used only to reply to you — nothing from the form is added to a list or shared with anyone else.

If you subscribe to the blog newsletter, your email goes to Buttondown, the newsletter service, which asks you to confirm it. It's used only to send you one short email when a new post goes up, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Buttondown keeps the list under its own privacy policy; IZZR Labs doesn't copy it anywhere else.

IZZR Labs doesn't sell, rent, or share any data.

Purchases and payments happen on Gumroad, under Gumroad's own privacy and payment policies — this site never sees your card details.

Questions about this page can go through the contact form.
